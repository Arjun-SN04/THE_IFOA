What is the IACRA and FTN Number? expand_more

IACRA (Integrated Airman Certification and Rating Application) is the FAA's online portal for certificates. The FTN (FAA Tracking Number) is your unique lifelong ID assigned during registration.

You MUST have an FTN to schedule knowledge exams and appoint an Agent for Service.

How do I find my FTN? expand_more

Login to your IACRA profile at iacra.faa.gov. Your FTN number is prominently displayed on your personal dashboard under your profile information.

Is appointing an agent mandatory? expand_more

Yes. As of July 7, 2025, all existing FAA certificate holders with a non-U.S. address must designate an agent to maintain valid certification status.

Can I appoint a corporate agent? expand_more

Yes, IFOA USA Corp serves as your official corporate Agent for Service, providing a permanent and reliable U.S. presence for all FAA-related matters.
